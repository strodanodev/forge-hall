The FORGE: about, and what it asks your wallet to do

A demo hall for Rapture trading cards, running on the LitVM Liteforge test network (chain 4441). The currency, zkLTC, is test currency: you can get free test zkLTC from the Liteforge faucet. Using the FORGE means you accept its terms of use.

Every request the app can make

ConnectShares your public address with this page. It cannot move funds or see your keys.
Add / switch networkAdds "LitVM Liteforge" (chain 4441, RPC liteforge.rpc.caldera.xyz) to your wallet and switches to it, if it is not there already.
Buy a packOne transaction to the PackShop contract: the price shown on the button (currently 0.001 zkLTC) plus a small network fee. Function buyPack().
Open the packA second transaction to the same contract (openPack), after a short wait. It mints 5 cards into your wallet. You pay only the network fee.
RefundOnly if a bought pack was never opened in time: refundExpired returns exactly what you paid.
Sign in (optional)Signs a plain text message (EIP-4361, "Sign in to the FORGE"). It is free, it is not a transaction, and it only proves you control the address.

What it never asks for

  • Token or NFT approvals (approve, setApprovalForAll) or permit / typed-data signatures.
  • Your seed phrase or private key. No site should ever ask for these.
  • Any transfer of tokens or NFTs you already own.
If your wallet ever shows one of the requests above, or a recipient other than the contract listed below, reject it.

Good to know

  • This is a test deployment. Pack contents are drawn from the chain's block hash, which is fine for a test network and is not meant to carry real value.
  • Cards are ordinary ERC-721 tokens in your wallet. Nothing here holds custody of them.
  • The page reads the chain directly from the public Liteforge RPC. It has no passwords; the optional sign-in stores your public address and a session cookie, nothing else.
